Named, checked, and dated

Arizona Commission on the Arts: Creative Capacity Grant

ARIZONA · GENERAL OPERATING SUPPORT · STATUS: FY2028 APPLICATIONS OPEN EARLY 2027

Through Creative Capacity Grants, the state provides general operating support to nonprofit organizations and Tribal Nations and Native-serving organizations in Arizona whose primary mission is to produce, present, teach, or serve the arts. The money covers day-to-day operating costs: salaries and personnel, artistic and production expenses, and administrative costs from office space to marketing. An organization's grant level is set by its annual budget, from at least $4,000 at the smallest levels to a minimum of $400,000 at the largest, and award amounts are projections set by the level, the funds available, geographic distribution priorities, and panel ranking; no fixed award range is published. Two rules shape the planning: the grant runs on an alternate-year cycle, with a full application once every two years and a lighter alternate-year application between, and the Commission limits an organization to one application per funding period across all of its grant programs, so this application rules out its Festival and other grants in the same period. The Fiscal Year 2027 period is closed; applications for Fiscal Year 2028 funding open in early 2027. Contact route: the Creative Capacity Grant page at azarts.gov and info@azarts.gov.

National Endowment for the Arts: Grants for Arts Projects

NATIONWIDE · TWO CYCLES A YEAR · STATUS: 2027 DEADLINES NOT YET PUBLISHED, REGISTRATION IS THE LONG LEAD

The federal arts project's grant funds arts projects, not general operating support, across disciplines that include Arts Education, Music, Visual and Media Arts, Local Arts Agencies, and Presenting and Multidisciplinary Works, and it expressly welcomes organizations serving rural communities. Challenge America applicants may request only $10,000; other applicants may request from $10,000 to $100,000, and a 1:1 cost share is required, which may include cash and in-kind contributions. Applications run in two parts, first through Grants.gov and then through the NEA's Applicant Portal, in a February cycle and a July cycle each year; the 2027 deadlines are not yet published. The gates to plan around: at least five years of arts programming before the deadline, at least $20,000 in operating expenses in the most recent fiscal year, and generally one application per calendar year. The preparation step with the longest lead time is registration: the NEA states that registering with Login.gov, SAM.gov, and Grants.gov can take several weeks, so an organization aiming at the next cycle starts there. Contact route: the discipline staff emails listed on the program's contacts page at arts.gov.

Arizona Community Foundation

ARIZONA · ARTS & CULTURE IS A NAMED FOCUS AREA · STATUS: ONE CYCLE OPENS NOVEMBER 30, 2026, MARICOPA COUNTY YOUTH PROGRAMS

The foundation awards grants year-round across Arizona and names Arts & Culture one of its areas of focus, in its words supporting a thriving arts scene and diverse cultural offerings in Arizona communities. Read its structure plainly: the foundation states that its competitive grants are awarded by invitation only, alongside discretionary grants funded by donor endowments and donor-advised grants aligned to each fund's purpose, and some competitive grants are administered by its regional affiliates, including offices for Flagstaff and Northern Arizona and for Gila County. That makes this a relationship and visibility funder more than an open-application one for most community arts organizations. The one open cycle listed on its grant opportunities page today is the Summer Youth Program Fund in Phoenix, opening November 30, 2026 and closing January 8, 2027, for summer enrichment programs for children and youth in Maricopa County, with arts and culture among its program areas. Award ranges are not published on the page; cycles are posted as they open. Contact route: grants@azfoundation.org.

Proof, from a funder's IRS filing

$7,400 for general operations

In its Form 990 for the fiscal year ending March 2025, the Arizona Community Foundation reported a grant of $7,400 to the Flagstaff Shakespeare Festival of Flagstaff, Arizona, designated as support for general operations. It is the pattern this guide is built on: foundation money reaches small community arts organizations in this state, at the scale those organizations actually run on.

Drawn from the foundation's IRS Form 990 filings (Schedule I) via ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, November 25, 2026.
