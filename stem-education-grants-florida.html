Named, checked, and dated

Motorola Solutions Foundation: Annual Grants Program

NATIONWIDE · TECHNOLOGY & ENGINEERING EDUCATION · STATUS: OPEN, INQUIRY DUE JANUARY 21, 2027

The foundation's giving has a narrow focus: first responder programming and technology and engineering education. The education side supports exactly the hands-on work this guide is for, in its words engaging students through activities like computer science, robotics, coding, AI and cybersecurity, and providing scholarships and workforce placement for under-resourced groups, with school-aged students ages 8 to 18 a stated priority. The 2027 grant cycle is officially open. The route is an eligibility quiz, then an inquiry form due January 21, 2027; selected organizations are notified by late March 2027 and invited to submit a full application by April 22, 2027, with decisions in August 2027. The maximum request is $50,000, with returning grant partners allowed to request more. The foundation also hosts informational calls on December 8, 9, and 10, 2026. One honest caveat: the foundation prioritizes named locations where its employees live and work, and its Florida priority location is South Florida, so a Sarasota program applies as a Florida organization outside the priority metros, on the strength of the program fit. Contact route: foundation@motorolasolutions.com.

Community Foundation of Sarasota County: Community Impact Grants

SARASOTA, MANATEE, CHARLOTTE & DESOTO COUNTIES · STATUS: OPENS DECEMBER 1, 2026, ABOUT A 30-DAY WINDOW

This is the closest geography fit of the three: eligibility requires at least one current program with local impact in Sarasota, Manatee, Charlotte, or DeSoto County. Community Impact Grants are designed, in the foundation's words, to support nonprofits that help families, children and households achieve lasting economic stability and mobility. It is a generalist community funder rather than a STEM-named one, so the case for a STEM program rests on education access: free and scholarship STEM programming, equipment, and curriculum for students who would otherwise go without is the kind of education access this purpose covers, and the foundation's own filings show education grants to school foundations and learning programs across its four counties. Grant requests should not exceed $25,000, and the average award is approximately $15,000. Prerequisites: an IRS determination letter showing 501(c)(3) public charity status, an approved and current profile in The Giving Partner, and no outstanding grant reports to the foundation. The cycle opens December 1, 2026 and stays open for approximately 30 days; the foundation runs a weekly Friday Zoom call for applicant questions and an information session on December 8. Contact route: impact@cfsarasota.org.

Walmart: Spark Good Local Grants

LOCAL, BY FACILITY · STATUS: OPEN NOW, CYCLE 3 RUNS TO DECEMBER 31, 2026

Local grants of $250 to $5,000 are awarded by individual Walmart stores, Sam's Clubs, and distribution centers to organizations serving their own communities, and the organization must serve the same service area as the facility it applies to. The current cycle runs from August 1 to December 31, 2026, with the last day to submit a proposal on the cycle's final day; two further cycles follow in the same fiscal year pattern. This is a general local funder, not a STEM program, and at this size the natural fit is direct program costs: robotics kits, parts, and equipment that put hands-on STEM in front of local students. The prerequisites come first: the organization must hold a Spark Good account on Walmart.com and be verified by Deed, Walmart's third-party verification partner, before it can apply. An organization may hold a maximum of 25 applications in pending or approved status at any time, and may receive one grant per facility per fiscal year. Contact route: the Spark Good local grants page at walmart.org.

Proof, from a funder's IRS filing

$13,475 for science education in Sarasota

In its Form 990 for the fiscal year ending June 2025, the Community Foundation of Sarasota County reported a grant of $13,475 to Minorities in Shark Sciences of Sarasota, Florida, for education. It is the pattern this guide is built on: the local foundation's money reaches science education organizations in its own counties, at the scale local programs actually run on.

Drawn from the foundation's IRS Form 990 filings (Schedule I) via ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, November 25, 2026.
