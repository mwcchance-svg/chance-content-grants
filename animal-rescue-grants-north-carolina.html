Animal rescue grants in North Carolina

For rescues and shelters placing animals in the Winston-Salem and Forsyth County area and across North Carolina: foster-based rescue, adoptions, spay and neuter work, and veterinary care, including care for hard-to-place animals. Three funders that fit that work, checked against their own published guidelines, plus one real past grant drawn from a funder's IRS filing. One of the three has a live deadline on January 5, 2027.

Named, checked, and dated

The Winston-Salem Foundation: Animal Welfare Grants

FORSYTH COUNTY · 2027 CYCLE OPEN NOW · CLOSES JANUARY 5, 2027 · STATUS: OPEN

The foundation runs a dedicated grant cycle for animal welfare nonprofits serving Forsyth County, North Carolina, and directs organizations serving animals to apply through this cycle rather than its other programs. Grants run up to $25,000 and fund capital improvement projects, capacity-building projects, and program support, including supplies for animal care, animal food pantries, emergency veterinary assistance, and vaccine clinics. The 2027 cycle opened in the foundation's Grants Portal on November 16, 2026 and closes January 5, 2027 at 5:00 PM, with funding decisions notified at the end of March. An organization based outside Forsyth County is eligible only for the portion of a project that impacts Forsyth County. Contact route: the Grants Portal at wsfoundation.org, or program officer Anna Brewer at abrewer@wsfoundation.org.

Petco Love

NATIONWIDE · ANNUAL CYCLES · STATUS: BETWEEN CYCLES, ONE PREP STEP GATES THE NEXT WINDOW

Petco Love invests in nonprofit organizations nationwide that create lifesaving impact, from shelters and rescue groups to veterinary providers. Its Animal Welfare Organizations grants are, in its words, for organizations whose primary purpose is sheltering and adoptions. The most recent published window ran February 1 to March 31, 2026, and the next window is not yet published; award amounts for this program are not published either. The step that decides eligibility comes first: to be considered for the cycle, an organization must have submitted its annual data report through the Partner Portal by the date the cycle closes, so filing that report is the work to do now. Two related programs run year round: Vaccinated & Loved, for organizations providing free vaccine services, and Shelter Pet Cancer Treatment, for partners treating shelter pets for cancer. Contact route: the grants page at petcolove.org and the Partner Portal.

Best Friends Animal Society: Rachael Ray No-Kill Excellence Grants

NATIONWIDE · LETTER OF INTEREST FIRST · STATUS: 2027 LOI WINDOW CLOSED, A PREPARATION TARGET

These grants award up to $60,000 to no-kill organizations, with the amount requested not exceeding 10% of the organization's operating budget. They fund two kinds of projects: expanding existing programs or launching a new one that saves more lives, and helping a specific shelter below a 90% save rate improve its rate. Foster-based organizations are an expressly eligible partner type. The gates are real: applicants must be Best Friends Network members in good standing with a save rate of 90% or higher, based in the United States. The 2027 Letter of Interest window is closed, with decisions announced by the end of December 2026, and the next opening date is not published; the preparation move is to confirm Network standing and save-rate data and sign up for the funder's grant alerts. Contact route: grants@bestfriends.org.

Proof, from a funder's IRS filing

$21,000 in one fiscal year

In its Form 990 for the fiscal year ending December 2024, The Winston-Salem Foundation reported grants totaling $21,000 to Brother Wolf Animal Rescue of Asheville, North Carolina, across six grants for varying purposes. It is the pattern this guide is built on: this funder's money reaches animal rescues in this state, in amounts a local organization can plan around.

Drawn from the foundation's IRS Form 990 filings (Schedule I) via ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, November 25, 2026.
