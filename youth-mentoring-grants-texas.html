The three funders

Named, checked, and open

The George Foundation

FORT BEND COUNTY · ROLLING, NO DEADLINES · STATUS: OPEN

The foundation grants only to 501(c)(3) organizations located in Fort Bend County, or nonprofits providing vital services to Fort Bend County residents. Under its Education priority it names Youth Development as an area of particular interest, alongside dropout prevention and K-12 public education. Every request begins with a Letter of Inquiry through the foundation's online Grants Portal; there are no deadlines, and the annual cycle runs November 1 to October 31. A Small Grant track covers requests of $25,000 and under on a streamlined process, and the foundation's recent grant distribution runs 46% at $25,000 or less, 38% between $25,001 and $100,000, and 16% over $100,000. Review typically takes three to four months. Contact route: the Grants Portal on the foundation's site, or grants@thegeorgefoundation.org.

The Meadows Foundation

STATEWIDE TEXAS · PROPOSALS ACCEPTED ANY TIME · STATUS: OPEN

The foundation grants throughout Texas, with specific programs and projects accounting for about 85% of its giving. Its published program areas include education and human services, the two areas a youth mentoring program speaks to. Proposals are accepted at any time through its online portal; the board considers requests in January, April, June, September, and November, and the cycle generally runs four to six months, so apply at least six months before funding is needed. One condition to check before applying: a 501(c)(3) public charity must have held its IRS designation for at least two years, or apply through a fiscal sponsor. Contact route: the application portal at mfi.org.

Walmart Spark Good Local Grants

$250 TO $5,000 PER FACILITY · CYCLE-BASED · STATUS: OPEN ON THE CYCLE CALENDAR

Individual Walmart stores, Sam's Clubs, and distribution centers award local grants of $250 to $5,000 to organizations serving the same area the facility serves, and the program is open to community purposes rather than a fixed list of program areas. The fiscal year 2027 cycles run February 1 to April 15, May 1 to July 15, and August 1 to December 31. An organization may receive one grant per facility per fiscal year and may hold up to 25 pending or approved applications. Applying requires a Spark Good account and verification through Walmart's partner, Deed. Contact route: the Spark Good Local Grants page at walmart.org, applying through the facility that serves your area.

Proof, from a funder's IRS filing

$5,000

In its Form 990-PF for calendar year 2024, The Meadows Foundation reported a grant of $5,000 to 2 Inspire Peace Inc of Dallas, Texas, toward support for increased capacity to offer social-emotional programs to children and adults in Dallas. Social-emotional programming sits at the center of mentoring work, and the grant is the size a small program can realistically win.
