Animal rescue grants in Arizona

For rescues and shelters across Arizona, including foster-based work in communities like Maricopa in Pinal County: rescue and rehoming, feral cat spay and neuter, adoptions, and veterinary care. Three funders that fit that work, checked against their own published guidelines, plus one real past grant drawn from a funder's IRS filing. None of the three windows is open today; each runs on a published annual calendar, and the preparation steps below are what decide who is ready when the windows open.

The three funders

Named, checked, and dated

Arizona Companion Animal Spay and Neuter Committee (AZCASNC)

ALL 15 ARIZONA COUNTIES · ANNUAL MAIL-IN CYCLE · STATUS: 2026/2027 AWARDS ANNOUNCED, NEXT ROUND ON THE ANNUAL CALENDAR

This state committee distributes Arizona's Spay and Neuter of Animals Fund, raised through Pet Friendly license plate sales, and states that it strives to fund each of the fifteen counties across Arizona. Grant funds are, in its words, strictly for sterilization costs, only, under three project types: Public Pets (Domesticated) Spay/Neuter, Free-roaming Cat (Feral Cat) Spay/Neuter, and Sheltered Pets Spay/Neuter. The maximum request is $10,000 per project. Applicants are municipal or tribal animal control agencies and 501(c)(3) animal welfare organizations, and applications are submitted by mail as complete packets. The 2026/2027 cycle ran on the committee's published calendar: applications postmarked by August 31, 2026, committee review in September and October, approval notices around October 30, 2026, and funds distributed December 2026 to January 2027. The next round follows the same annual pattern, so the preparation work is assembling the packet well before the August postmark deadline. Contact route: the funding guidelines and grant process pages at azpetplates.org.

Petco Love

NATIONWIDE · ANNUAL CYCLES · STATUS: BETWEEN CYCLES, ONE PREP STEP GATES THE NEXT WINDOW

Petco Love invests in nonprofit organizations nationwide that create lifesaving impact, from shelters and rescue groups to veterinary providers. Its Animal Welfare Organizations grants are, in its words, for organizations whose primary purpose is sheltering and adoptions, and its application instructions list foster-based rescues among the eligible organization types. The most recent published window ran February 1 to March 31, 2026, and the next window is not yet published; award amounts for this program are not published either. The step that decides eligibility comes first: to be considered for the cycle, an organization must have submitted its annual data report through the Partner Portal by the date the cycle closes, so filing that report is the work to do now. A separate Access to Care cycle serves spay/neuter clinics, low-cost providers, and trap-neuter-return work; its most recent window ran September 1 to 30, 2026. Two further programs run year round: Vaccinated & Loved and Shelter Pet Cancer Treatment. Contact route: the grants page at petcolove.org and the Partner Portal.

Best Friends Animal Society: Rachael Ray No-Kill Excellence Grants

NATIONWIDE · LETTER OF INTEREST FIRST · STATUS: 2027 LOI WINDOW CLOSED, A PREPARATION TARGET

These grants award up to $60,000 to no-kill organizations, with the amount requested not exceeding 10% of the organization's operating budget. They fund two kinds of projects: expanding existing programs or launching a new one that saves more lives, and helping a specific shelter below a 90% save rate improve its rate. Foster-based organizations are an expressly eligible partner type. The gates are real: applicants must be Best Friends Network members in good standing with a save rate of 90% or higher, based in the United States. The 2027 Letter of Interest window is closed, with decisions announced by the end of December 2026, and the next opening date is not published; the preparation move is to confirm Network standing and save-rate data and sign up for the funder's grant alerts. Contact route: grants@bestfriends.org.

Proof, from a funder's IRS filing

$30,000 to one Arizona rescue

In its Form 990 for the fiscal year ending September 2025, Best Friends Animal Society reported a grant of $30,000 to the Arizona Animal Welfare League of Phoenix, Arizona, for program service support. It is the pattern this guide is built on: national funder money reaches Arizona animal welfare organizations, in amounts a local organization can plan around.

Drawn from the funder's IRS Form 990 filings (Schedule I) via ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, November 25, 2026.
